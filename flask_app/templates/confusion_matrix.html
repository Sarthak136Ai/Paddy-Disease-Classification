Confusion Matrix Structural Analysis

Dominant True-Positive Diagonal

The strong diagonal coloration reflects consistent per-class sensitivity across distinct disease phenotypes. In transfer learning models (ResNet50 & EfficientNet-B0), classes with distinct morphological features such as Tungro (orange leaf discoloration), Hispa (white linear feeding scars), and Normal (unblemished epidermis) show near-perfect true positive rates (>97%).

Inter-Bacterial Lesion Confusion

Minor off-diagonal confusion occurs between Bacterial Leaf Blight and Bacterial Leaf Streak. Both pathologies exhibit elongated chlorotic streaks and water-soaked leaf margins in early progression stages, leading to occasional feature overlap.

Fungal Blast & Brown Spot Separation

Diamond-shaped lesions of Blast (Pyricularia oryzae) and circular spots of Brown Spot (Bipolaris oryzae) are accurately separated by deep residual layers capturing high-frequency perimeter gradients and halo transitions.
